Name

Letters, digits, spaces and dashes. The name also becomes the scale's address: "Kitchen Scale" is http://kitchen-scale.local

Zigbee

—

Zigbee reports at the sampling rate above. The floor is the one exception: relaying continuous sampling takes the shared radio away from Wi-Fi. Change it from the console with "z <seconds>", or "z 0" to follow the sampling rate exactly. Counters since the scale last restarted. Rejoins climbing on their own, or refused reports, point at a link that is dropping even while this page still looks fine.

Wi-Fi

—

Joining your home Wi-Fi lets you open this page at its .local address (see Name) from your normal network. Leave the name empty and press Join to forget it.
