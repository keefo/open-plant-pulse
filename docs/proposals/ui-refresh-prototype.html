Open Plant Pulse — where the dated feeling comes from

Measured, not guessed: 124 distinct hex colours (ten are tokens), 26 font sizes mostly between 10 and 13px, eight corner radii, 61 hairline borders against 16 shadows, and no dark mode. The look isn't wrong — there is no system underneath it, so every screen re-decides.

What actually changed

and what deliberately did not

Changed

  • Light, not lines. 61 hairlines → shadows and tinted surfaces.
  • One type scale, 12·13·15·16·20·27·40. Nothing at 9px.
  • Three radii (10 · 14 · pill) instead of eight.
  • Two colour ramps with semantic names, so dark mode is a flip.
  • System sans, tabular figures. No Gill Sans fallback.
  • The graph paper goes. It reads as 2013 skeuomorphism.
  • Water left becomes a bar — a quantity drawn as a quantity.

Kept

  • The writing. "Water in the pot", "Nothing reported yet". It is the best thing here.
  • The density. This is a console, not a brochure.
  • The green. Deepened slightly, same identity.
  • The photograph first on a card — it is how you tell two pothos apart.
  • Serif, but earned: display numbers and the plant's own name, not every heading.
