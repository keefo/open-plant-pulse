# Home Assistant integration

Open Plant Pulse supports two integration paths. Direct BTHome is available once
sensor BLE advertising works. Hub-mediated MQTT is planned and adds durable
history and derived signals; it is not implemented yet.

## Path A: Direct BTHome

## Prerequisites

Home Assistant needs a working Bluetooth adapter or an ESPHome Bluetooth proxy
within range. Open Plant Pulse uses unencrypted BTHome v2 advertisements, so it
does not need Wi-Fi credentials, an MQTT broker, or a pairing code.

## Discovery

1. Complete hardware bring-up over USB and confirm plausible serial readings.
2. Flash firmware with BLE advertising enabled.
3. Open **Settings > Devices & services** in Home Assistant.
4. Select the discovered BTHome device and assign it to the plant's area.
5. Rename entities only after confirming their units and values.

Contract v1 exposes soil temperature in degrees C, soil moisture in percent, and
conductivity in microS/cm. Planned SHT45 support adds air temperature and air
relative humidity in contract v2. Entity IDs are generated by Home Assistant and
duplicate temperature measurements may receive positional suffixes; verify which
entity is soil versus air before creating automations.

## Path B: Desktop hub over MQTT

The future hub adapter will publish Home Assistant MQTT discovery, availability,
raw readings, and selected derived metrics. Home Assistant must remain optional:
BLE ingestion and SQLite writes continue when the broker is unavailable.

Do not configure this path until the M3 roadmap milestone is implemented and its
topic/device-identity contract is documented.

## Automation example

Copy and adapt [the example package](../examples/home-assistant/automations.yaml).
Thresholds are placeholders, not universal horticultural advice. Establish each
plant's baseline from its substrate, pot, probe position, and normal wet/dry
cycle before enabling notifications.

## Privacy and availability

Unencrypted BTHome data can be read by anyone within BLE range. It contains no
account credentials, but measurements and device presence are observable. A
30-minute sampling interval also means Home Assistant may take one or more cycles
to discover the device and should not mark it unavailable too aggressively.

## Troubleshooting

- No device: confirm the proxy sees other BLE devices and move it closer.
- Temperature only: verify BTHome object ordering and Home Assistant version.
- Swapped soil/air labels: verify the duplicate-temperature order against the
    active protocol contract and rename entities explicitly in Home Assistant.
- Implausible values: check the probe model's register order and scaling.
- Intermittent values: inspect RS485 polarity, termination, power stabilization,
  and advertisement duration before increasing radio power.
